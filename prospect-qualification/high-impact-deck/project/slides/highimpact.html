Revenue bar: incremental = online revenue × program lift · Test bar: two-proportion z-test, 80% power · 5

High-impact is defined by two bars. First, the program should be worth 25 to 50 million dollars a year. Nearly every order passes through cart, so incremental revenue is roughly online revenue times the conversion lift the program achieves. At a realistic 10% a year, that takes 250 to 500 million in online revenue. Second, the customer should be able to run five or more cart and checkout experiments a month, each reading a 5% lift in two weeks. With cart pages converting to orders at about 35%, that takes roughly 110 thousand unique cart visitors a month, which works out to about 10 thousand online orders a week.
