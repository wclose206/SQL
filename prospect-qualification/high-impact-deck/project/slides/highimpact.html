Revenue bar: incremental = online revenue × program lift ($250M+ brands reach $25–50M) · Test bar: two-proportion z-test, 80% power · 5

High-impact is defined by two bars. First, the brand needs at least 150 million dollars in online revenue. Nearly every order passes through cart, so incremental revenue is roughly online revenue times the conversion lift the program achieves: 15 million at a strong 10% a year, 30 million at an exceptional 20%. Brands above 250 million reach 25 to 50 million. Second, the customer should be able to run five or more cart and checkout experiments a month, each reading a 5% lift in two weeks. With cart pages converting to orders at about 35%, that takes roughly 110 thousand unique cart visitors a month, which works out to about 10 thousand online orders a week.
