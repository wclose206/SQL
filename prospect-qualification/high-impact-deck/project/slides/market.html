To find high-impact brands in the market, start with a ranked list of online retailers such as the Digital Commerce 360 Top 2000. Keep those with 150 million dollars or more in online sales, roughly 500. Set aside the largest fifty or so, which typically run their own experimentation teams. Then keep only brands whose revenue divided by AOV gives at least 10,000 orders a week; this removes most furniture, luxury and B2B. At the 150-million floor that means an AOV under about 290 dollars. That leaves an estimated 300 target accounts. These counts are interpolated from public ranges and should be confirmed with the paid database.
