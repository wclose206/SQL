This is the slide to keep. The Tier 1 ICP is the floor: below 4,500 online orders a week, we don't engage at all. The high-impact ICP is where we focus new business: about 10,000 orders a week, roughly 110 thousand monthly cart visitors, and 250 million dollars or more in online revenue. That combination supports five or more cart and checkout experiments a month and a program worth 25 million dollars or more a year.
